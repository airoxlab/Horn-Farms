Show pigs

Show pigs for 4-H and FFA projects

Horn Farms raises show pigs alongside the cattle. Litters, breeds and timing change from year to year, so the best way to find out what's ready is a quick call or text.

  • Ask what's on the ground now and when the next pigs will be ready.
  • Tell them your fair date so you get a pig that fits your weigh-in.
  • Set up a time to see the pigs in person at the farm.
Freezer pork, now and thenThe farm sometimes has pork for the freezer. In June 2026 it offered a half hog processed at Rodabaugh Bros. Call to ask if any is available.

How to buy

Picking your next project

There's no online catalog. You deal with the family directly, and the farm says it cares about building good relationships with the families it sells to.

  1. Call or text

    Reach Horn Farms at (419) 722-1539 and say whether you're after a steer, a heifer or a pig.

  2. Share your show plans

    Your county fair, the weigh-in dates and any breed or class rules for your project.

  3. Come see them

    Set a time to look the animals over at the farm on State Route 37 in Arlington.

  4. Follow along

    Available animals get posted on the Horn Farms Facebook page.

Good questions to ask

  • What steers, heifers and pigs are available right now?
  • What are the birth months and pedigrees?
  • Which shows would each one fit best?
  • How do pricing, deposits and pickup work?

New to showing?

First-year 4-H and FFA families are welcome to call with questions. Tell the family where you're starting from and what your fair requires, and they can point you toward an animal that fits.

Ask about the details directly; this page doesn't list prices or sale dates.
