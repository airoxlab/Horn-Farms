# Horn Farms – Show Cattle & Show Pigs

A one-page sample website for Horn Farms, a show cattle and show pig operation in Arlington, Ohio.

- **Phone:** (419) 722-1539 (Facebook lists it as a mobile number, so the site also offers a "Send a text" link)
- **Address:** 15234 State Route 37, Arlington, OH 45814 (Hancock County). Plus code: WCWF+7W Arlington
- **Google listing:** "Horn Farms - Show Cattle & Show Pigs", category *Cattle farm*. The listing is unclaimed and has no reviews, photos, hours or website.
- **Facebook:** https://www.facebook.com/HornFarms/ (category "Pet service", about 172 followers, 1 review with no text visible to logged-out visitors)

## Structure

- `index.html`: the whole site. CSS is inline, there's no build step, and it has light and dark themes.
- Fonts: Ultra (a heavy slab, like a show-bill poster) for display, with Sofia Sans and Sofia Sans Extra Condensed for body text and labels.
- Palette: taken from the farm's own roadside sign. Sign navy, a sunset-gold oval, an ember-orange pinstripe, and burning-bush red.
- `img/`: eight photos from the farm's Facebook page and one stock image.

## Sections

1. Sticky header with a call button
2. Split hero: the "Horn Farms Show Stock" roadside sign and the farm's own tagline, quoted word for word
3. Info strip: what they raise, the address, phone and Facebook
4. Show cattle: five calf photos, two past pedigrees the farm posted (Guts and Glory × Unstoppable; Business done Right × Naughty Pine), and a quote from a 2020 post
5. Show pigs: honest "call for what's available" copy and a freezer-pork note from a June 2026 post (a half hog processed at Rodabaugh Bros)
6. Show ring: Reserve Champion Market Steer and Reserve Champion Beef Feeder at the 2017 Hancock County Fair, both from the farm's own Sale of Champions photos
7. How to buy: four steps and a list of questions to ask
8. Find us: address card and an embedded Google map
9. Call/text band and a footer with data sources

## Data sources and honesty

- The facts come from Google Maps and the public (logged-out) Facebook page. PetTrader lists the farm too, but adds nothing beyond the address and phone. No website, Instagram or online sale-barn listing (ShowPigs.net, Sullivan Supply and others) turned up.
- The 2017 ribbons are documented by the farm's captioned posts ("Trenton's Reserve champion steer", "Jenna's Reserve champion feeder calf") and by the signs visible in the photos. Exhibitor first names are left off the site because they are likely minors.
- Not invented: breeds, pig genetics, prices, sale dates, online sales, current availability, history or testimonials. The Facebook review has no visible text, so there's nothing to quote.
- Photos: the sign, five calf portraits and two fair photos all come from the Horn Farms Facebook page. Four of the calf portraits carry a "Farmstead Images" photographer watermark. The pig photo is Unsplash stock (photo-1545468258-576dbac5faa9). It is labelled on the page and in the footer as representative.
- Not used: a grilled pork chop photo (possibly stock), a low-resolution light-coloured calf and a distant calf shot.
- Excluded businesses with similar names: Horn Farms (Pleasantville, OH, crops and hay), Horn Family Farm Market (Thornville), Horn Family Ag LLC, Van Horn Farms, Horn Acres (Knox County), Horn Livestock (Anson, TX), Horn Cattle Co. (Wes Horn), and Horn Farm (@hornfarm, an ecology center).

The Sendra tracking script is included before `</body>`.
